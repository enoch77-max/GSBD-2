Battery Safety & Handling Guide

Essential DIY safety rules for lithium-ion and LiFePO4 pack builders.

Never Solder Directly to 18650 / 21700 Cells

Soldering directly on cell terminals heats the internal chemical jelly roll and destroys the CID (current interrupt device) safety seal. Always use a spot welder with pure nickel strip or pre-soldered cell holders.

Golden Rules for Safe Builds
Always install a matching BMS: Never run or charge series cells without a battery management system to prevent over-charge, over-discharge, and thermal runaway.
Check cell voltages before parallel connection: All cells must be within 0.05V of each other before welding in parallel to prevent massive equalizing inrush currents.
Use Barley Paper (Fish Paper) Insulator Rings: The positive terminal collar on 18650s is very close to the negative outer can. Always add an adhesive insulator ring before spot-welding nickel.
Use the correct charger: LiFePO4 cells need 3.65V per cell (14.6V for 4S). Li-ion cells need 4.20V per cell (12.6V for 3S). Never use a lead-acid charger without lithium profile.
