Warranty & Return Policy

Transparent guarantees and local warranty service in Dhaka.

Warranty Coverage Summary
7-Day Dead-On-Arrival (DOA) Replacement: If any cell, circuit board, or sensor arrives defective, we replace it immediately free of charge.
Cell Capacity Guarantee: Genuine cells (Samsung, LG, Molicel, EVE) are guaranteed to test within ±5% of manufacturer factory ratings on professional analyzers.
Custom Packs & Router UPS: 1-Year to 3-Year local service warranty on spot welds and BMS boards at our Elephant Road workshop.
What is NOT covered?

Damage caused by short-circuiting terminals, reverse-polarity wiring, discharging below 2.0V, charging without a proper CC-CV lithium charger or BMS, or soldering directly on cell terminals with a high-heat iron (which damages internal safety vents).
